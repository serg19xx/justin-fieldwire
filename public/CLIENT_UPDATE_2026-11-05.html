FieldWire — group SMS and email fixed

November 5, 2026. All changes are live.

In short: group SMS and group email no longer time out, and the same message is never sent twice to the same person. If a send is interrupted, just press Send again: it continues with the people who have not received it yet.

What happened

  • The system tried to send the whole group in one go. After 15 seconds the page gave up and showed "timeout of 15000ms exceeded", and the server stopped in the middle of the list.
  • SMS (Nov 4, evening): each attempt reached about 75 people before stopping, and every new attempt started again from the top of the list. 335 people received the text; about 100 of them received it 2 to 8 times. 9 numbers were rejected as invalid (missing digits or country code).
  • Email (Nov 5, 5:44 AM): 276 people received the email once, then the send stopped. The rest of the selected clients did not get it.
  • You received the email yourself because justin.kearney@me.com is saved as the email of pharmacist record #2 in your client list. This was not an error; that record was simply part of the selection.

What we changed

  • Sending in small batches. Large groups are now sent 25 at a time, with progress on screen, so the page no longer times out.
  • No duplicates. The system remembers who received what for 7 days:
    • SMS: the same text is not sent again to the same phone number.
    • Email: an email with the same subject is not sent again to the same address.
    • If two clients share a phone number or email, it is sent only once.
  • Yesterday's and today's sends are already recorded. The 335 phones and 276 email addresses above will not get the same SMS or email again.
  • Each send finishes properly on the server, even if the connection drops.

How to use it

  1. Refresh the page once (Cmd + Shift + R on Mac, Ctrl + F5 on Windows).
  2. Select the clients and click Send SMS or Send email as before.
  3. While sending you will see Sending 125 / 356… Keep this window open until it finishes. Keep the window open until it closes by itself.
  4. At the end you get a summary, for example SMS: 21 sent, 9 failed, 335 skipped (already received it). "Failed" means no phone/email or an invalid one. "Skipped" means the person already got this message.
  5. If the send stops for any reason, just press Send again. People who already received it are skipped automatically.

To finish the campaigns you started

  • Email: select the same clients and send the email with exactly the same subject ("Pharmacy Leasing Opportunity – Join a Growing Medical Hub"). Only the people who did not receive it will get it.
  • SMS: almost everyone selected already received it. Sending exactly the same text to the same selection reaches only those who did not.
Good to know:
  • To send a new message to the same people within 7 days, change the SMS text or the email subject.
  • Please fix the invalid phone numbers in the client cards (for example 647008687 is missing a digit).
  • If you do not want your own copy, remove or change the email on pharmacist record #2.
