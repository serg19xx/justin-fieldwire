FieldWire — attendance is now tracked automatically

November 6, 2026. All changes are live.

In short: you no longer need to watch who forgot to clock in or out. Workers get reminders by SMS, paid times are set by clear rules, a day without End day closes by itself, and you get a short summary every morning and evening. Everything that needs your decision is on one new page: Planning → Attendance.

How paid time is set

The worker's real Start day / End day times (with photo and location) are always kept. The paid time follows these rules. Example shift: 8:00 – 17:00.

What the worker doesPaid timeWhat you see
Start day before 8:15 (also if earlier than 8:00)From 8:00—
Start day after 8:15From the real time"Late start"
No Start day at allNothing is filled in"Not clocked in"
End day before 17:00Until the real time"Left early"
End day after 17:00Until 17:00If more than 15 min late: "Overtime to approve"
No End day by 18:00Day closes at 17:00"Did not clock out"

Overtime is paid only after you approve it. Times you change yourself are never overwritten by the rules, and locked days are not touched. Weekends work the same way as weekdays.

What workers get

  • Start reminders: up to 3 SMS, 15, 30 and 45 minutes after the shift start, until they tap Start day.
  • End reminders: up to 3 SMS, 15, 30 and 45 minutes after the shift end, until they tap End day. The SMS has a link Still working? Tap: … with three buttons: +1 hour, +2 hours or Until I finish (no more reminders and no automatic close until End day). The same buttons are on their Timesheet under Working late?
  • Second site in one day: if your schedule sends a worker to another site later in the day, they get one SMS 15 minutes after the planned start there, with a link to confirm arrival. They can also tap Arrived on their Timesheet. The day is still paid as one span from the first Start day to the last End day.

What you get

  • 9:00 AM SMS — only if someone has not clocked in or started late, for example FieldWire attendance Nov 6: Not clocked in: Dale B. Review: …
  • 7:00 PM SMS + email — only if there were problems that day. The email has a table per worker: site, shift, clock in / out, paid time and the list of issues.
  • No problems — no messages.
  • In Schedule, paid times set by the rules are marked blue Auto; times you changed are marked Edited.

How to fix a day

  1. Refresh the page once (Cmd + Shift + R on Mac, Ctrl + F5 on Windows).
  2. Open Planning → Attendance, or tap the link in the SMS or email. Choose Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days or any dates. By default only days with issues are shown.
  3. For each worker you see the shift, the sites, the phone times and the issues. Then:
    • Save — after changing Paid start, Paid end or the payroll note.
    • Approve overtime — pays until the worker's real End day.
    • Left early at paid end — when someone left without tapping End day: type the time they left in Paid end, then press it.
    • Accept as is — keep the times shown and clear the issues.
    • Lock day — final for payroll; nobody can change the day until you unlock it.
Good to know:
  • The rules start from today. Older days without End day (for Dale: Oct 28 – Nov 4) were not closed automatically; please review them in Attendance with Last 7 days or custom dates.
  • For the arrival reminder at a second site, that site needs an expected start time in the published schedule.
  • Summaries go to both of your accounts (project manager and admin), so the evening email may arrive twice.
  • Reminders use the expected start and finish times from the published schedule. If you change them, reminders follow automatically.
