Schedule & worker timesheet — testing guide

For the product owner / client. Plain language. Covers manager Schedule (payroll notebook, Week / Month / Custom periods), the worker Timesheet (Start day / End day, SMS reminders) and marking tasks done (Ready For Inspection).

Plan length: test over 2–3 real work days. Start day and End day only work for today (real time, no backdating). A short interval (Start day, then End day an hour later) is enough — full 8-hour days are not required.

1. Two apps — what each is for

A) Manager app (you / PM)

Desktop-style app after login as project manager or admin.

  • Gantt / Tasks — the work plan (what to build, when, who is on the task).
  • Schedule (menu: Planning → Schedule) — your payroll notebook for the day: where the person goes (project + address), expected hours, optional KM. You assign the project by hand for that day (from memory, notes, or a glance at Gantt). This is not the same system as tasks.

Why separate? Pay days and job-site destinations are your decision for payroll. The app does not invent that from Gantt automatically.

B) Worker app (field crew)

Mobile-first app after login as worker / foreman / contractor (task role).

  • Tasks — projects with their tasks from the plan (Gantt), split into Open and Closed. Each task shows only its scheduled time (for example 08:00 – 17:00, 9 h/day). There is no clock in/out on tasks.
  • Mark as done — the circle next to a task. The app asks for a photo of the finished work (can be skipped) and sets the task to Ready For Inspection. Only the PM completes it or re-opens it.
  • Hours (bottom tab) — the Timesheet: Start day / End day once per day, and the month view. Paid hours = End day − Start day − 30 min unpaid break (automatic).
  • SMS reminders — if the worker has not tapped Start day 5 min after the scheduled start of their tasks for today, or has not tapped End day 5 min after the scheduled end, they get a text with a link to the Timesheet.
Remember: Manager Schedule ≠ Worker Hours. Same idea (time + place), different purpose — so you are not looking at a duplicate of your own model.

Day notes — message for the day (not a task)

On the manager Schedule, Day notes are a short message from you for that worker/day/jobsite (materials, contacts, site context). Workers see today’s notes at the top of their Tasks home screen — not inside a Task screen — so the payroll notebook stays separate from Tasks.

2. Manager Schedule periods — Week vs Month / Custom

On Planning → Schedule you can switch the period:

ModeWhat it is forCan you edit?
Week Plan one calendar week (Mon–Sun): destinations, Expected times, Save draft, Publish, Reopen Yes — only here
Month Review many days at once; see Actual hours total for the month No — review only
Custom Same as Month, but you pick From / To (up to ~62 days) No — review only
How to edit when you are looking at a month: use the banner button Edit in Week view (or tap Week). If that week shows Published, click Reopen week for editing, change days, then Save draft / Publish. Repeat for the next week if needed. Month and Custom stay for browsing and the hours total.

Why Month / Custom cannot be edited (product decision)

The plan is stored per calendar week, with a clear lifecycle: Draft → Publish → (optional) Reopen → Draft again. Workers see the last published week until you publish again.

A month (or a custom range) always covers several weeks, and those weeks can mix Draft and Published status. There is no single “Publish this month” or “Reopen this month” without breaking that week model.

If the request sounds like: “I also want to edit inside Month and Custom” — that conflicts with the week Draft / Publish / Reopen rules already agreed for payroll planning.

What we deliver instead (no contradiction):
  • Variable period (Week / Month / Custom) for review and Actual hours sum.
  • Editing only in Week, one week at a time, with Reopen when published.

Building true month/custom editing would mean a different product: editing many weeks in one screen, deciding which weeks to reopen/save/publish together, and new conflict rules across week boundaries. That is a separate scope — not a small switch on the current Schedule.

3. What to prepare (once, ~15 minutes)

  1. Task on Gantt covering the next 2–3 days, with Start / End time set (e.g. 08:00 – 17:00); worker assigned to the task.
  2. Worker profile has a mobile phone number (for SMS reminders).
  3. Worker phone: logged in to the worker app.

4. Multi-day test plan

Day 1 — morning

  1. Do nothing until 5 minutes after the scheduled start → Expect: SMS “Please clock in (Start day)”.
  2. Worker: Hours → Start day. Expect: “Working since …”.

Day 1 — during the day

  1. Worker: Tasks → project → tap the circle next to a task → take a photo (or “Mark done without photo”).
  2. Expect: the task is crossed out with “Ready For Inspection · waiting for PM”; it stays in Open until the PM acts.
  3. You (PM): open the task → set Completed (it moves to the worker’s Closed tab) or set it back to a working status (the worker can mark it done again).

Day 1 — evening

  1. Do nothing until 5 minutes after the scheduled end → Expect: SMS “Please clock out (End day)”.
  2. Worker: Hours → End day. Expect: day shows start – end and hours with the 30 min break deducted (e.g. 08:00 – 16:30 → 8 h).

Day 2–3 — normal days

  • Start day / End day on time → no SMS reminders that day.
  • Hours month view: each day shows the work day hours and the tasks with their scheduled time; Total sums paid hours.

6. Short “why” (for conversation, not for programmers)

  • Your Schedule — notebook for pay: where they went that day by your decision, expected hours.
  • Week — the only place to change the notebook and publish it.
  • Month / Custom — look across days and sum Actual hours; not a second editor.
  • Tasks / Gantt — plan of the job.
  • Worker Hours — their timesheet: Start day / End day, minus the 30 min break; they cannot edit past days.
  • Ready For Inspection — the crew says “done”, the PM decides “completed”.
