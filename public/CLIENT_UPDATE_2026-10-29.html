Summary of today's updates for the project manager and the foreman. All changes are live.

In short:
  • Schedule: days no longer disappear when you edit. Past days stay editable until you lock them, and you can correct the actual hours and add a payroll note for each day.
  • All 9 items from your change list are done: task priority, pictures on tasks, a real Photos section, an SMS when a task is delayed, and the foreman's sorting, status changes and read-only Plans / Reports / Team.

1. Schedule — days going missing, and manual day lock for payroll

What was wrong

The Schedule only allowed planning forward. After you reopened a published week, every day before today blocked Save and Publish, and the page asked you to "Clear past-day assignments". That button, and any save afterwards, deleted those days. This is why Monday and Tuesday were empty for Dale.

Already fixed for you: Dale's planned days for Monday Oct 26 (Optimal) and Tuesday Oct 27 (Kingsmead) are restored with their day notes. Kilometres and expected times for those two days were not saved in the backup copy, so please re-enter them if you need them. His phone clock-in / clock-out records were never lost and show again on those days.

How it works now

  • Days never lock by themselves. Yesterday, last week, any past day stays editable until a project manager locks it.
  • Lock day / Unlock day. Each day (today and earlier) of the selected worker has a Lock day link. Lock it when the day is accurate for payroll. A locked day shows a grey Locked badge. Nothing about it can change, and the worker cannot clock in or out on it. You can unlock it at any time.
  • Correct the actual hours. Act start and Act end are now editable for you. Type the real time and it saves immediately. The time from the worker's phone is kept and shown under your correction (for example "Edited · phone 19:02"). Total hours worked uses your corrected times.
  • Payroll note for every day. Under each day there is a yellow box for a payroll note, for example "Dale did not come in" or "Left at 2 pm, approved". It saves as soon as you click outside the box. You can add it even on a day with nothing planned.
  • No Save draft needed for payroll. Corrected times, notes and locks save right away. They also work on published weeks and in the Month and Custom views, which is handy for a payroll period.
  • Past weeks can be reopened and edited like the current week.

Example: "Today, Dale is not coming in"

  1. Open Planning → Schedule and select Dale in the Worker list.
  2. In today's row, type "Not coming in today" in the yellow payroll note box.
  3. Tomorrow and later, the day is still open: you can adjust times or the note if anything changes.
  4. When the week is checked for payroll, click Lock day on each day that is correct.
Good to know: the X on a past day still removes that day's plan (destination and day notes). It now asks for confirmation first, and the actual times and payroll note are kept. Thursday Oct 29 for Dale currently shows Shed Completion. If it should be 208 Vic, just change it.

4. Security, sign-in and other fixes

  • Project data is private to the project. Plans and the project team are now visible only to admins, project managers, people working on that project and the clients linked to it. Only admins and project managers can upload, move or delete plan files or change the team. Before, any signed-in user could reach this data.
  • Personal details protected. Date of birth, nationality and emergency contacts in the team list are visible only to admins and project managers. Others see the name, role, phone and email.
  • Contractor portal photos fixed. The photo list and photo download in the contractor portal work again, and deleted photos no longer appear there.
  • Sign-in. If someone has not opened the app for more than 7 days, they now see the sign-in screen straight away instead of a half-working page. Everyone else will not notice any change.
  • Automatic sign-out after inactivity is now 12 hours (was 30 minutes), so the crew does not have to sign in again during the day.
  • General clean-up and stability fixes across the app.
