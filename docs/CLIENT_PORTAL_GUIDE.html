1. Self-Serve Account Registration

Clients (Doctors and Pharmacists) can register their own accounts without administrative intervention, provided their contact records already exist in the system's CRM database.

How It Works:

  1. Navigate to the Login page and click on the Client (MD / Rx) tab.
  2. Click on the Request Account Access sub-tab.
  3. Select your role: Doctor (Physician) or Pharmacist.
  4. Enter your registered Email, Cell Phone, and create a password.
  5. Verification Logic: The system matches the last 10 digits of the phone and lowercase email against the CRM tables:
    • Doctors are matched against fw_physician.
    • Pharmacists are matched against fw_pharmacist.

2. Doctor Portal (Primary Client Access)

Purpose: Designed for Doctors to track the step-by-step progress of their clinic construction, view completed milestones, and prepare for the grand opening.

Project Isolation

Doctors can only see projects where they are assigned as the Primary Client.

View-Only Mode

All creation, editing, and deletion controls are hidden. Strictly read-only.

Available Sections:

  • 📁 Profile: High-level project details, start/end dates, and status.
  • 📁 Plans & Drawings: Access to project documents. Security Filtered (Only folders containing "Documents", "Drawings", or "Resources MD" are visible).
  • 📁 Tasks: Outstanding and completed tasks, assignees, and dates.
  • 📁 Calendar: Interactive calendar showing milestones.
  • 📁 Photos: Visual updates and site photos.
  • 📁 Reports & Settings: Read-only reports and project settings.

3. Pharmacist Portal (Marketplace & Secondary Access)

Pharmacists have two distinct access modes depending on their relationship with the project.

Mode A: Location Marketplace (Pharmacy Access 1)

For pharmacists looking for a new clinic location to partner with.

  • 🔍 Status Filtering: Only projects with status "Actively Looking For A Location" or "Securing Location" are visible.
  • 🔒 Data Masking (Pre-NDA): Exact Address, Client Name, and Financials are completely hidden until an NDA is signed.
  • 🤝 4-Step Pursuit Workflow:

    Step 1: Type full legal name to sign the digital NDA.

    Step 2: System automatically generates a signed PDF and uploads it to the project files.

    Step 3: Address and Client Name are instantly revealed.

    Step 4: Review, sign, or propose modifications to the Short Lease Agreement.

Mode B: Project Partner (Pharmacy Access 2 / Secondary Client)

For pharmacists officially attached to an active project as a partner.

  • 📊 Live Analytics: Interactive charts showing project metrics.
  • 📁 Drawings & Resources: Access to project documents. Security Filtered (Only folders containing "Drawings", "Pharmacy", or "Resources" are visible).
  • 📅 Calendar & Photos: View timeline, milestones, and site photos.
